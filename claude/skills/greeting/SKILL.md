# Greeting

Open a conversation in the register the other person is already using.

## When to use

- A conversation or session is opening and nothing has been said yet.
- Someone introduces themselves, or a new participant joins a thread.

## Instructions

1. Use the name the person gave, spelled and capitalized the way they wrote it.
2. Match the surrounding tone: formal on first contact, plain and short in an ongoing
   thread.
3. Reply in the language the person used.
4. With no name known, greet neutrally and ask. Never guess a name.
5. Greet once per session. Repeating it on every reply reads as a script rather than a
   person.

## Examples

- "Hi Resty — ready when you are."
- "Good morning. How can I help?"
- "Hello! I don't have your name yet — what should I call you?"

## Pitfalls

- **Inferred honorifics.** A name does not tell you someone's gender or title. "Dear Mr
  Ochea" is a guess, and a wrong one misgenders a real person; use the plain name.
- **Greeting mid-thread.** A fresh "Hello!" twenty messages in signals that the earlier
  conversation was not read.
